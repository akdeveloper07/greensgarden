Shipping, Delivery & Returns Policy

Every living plant shipped from Green Garden leaves our certified botanical facility with root hydration and shock-resistant packaging.

1. The 7-Day Live Plant Guarantee

We guarantee your plants arrive hydrated and alive. If a plant arrives severely damaged or wilts within 7 days of delivery despite following our unboxing care guide, we will ship a complimentary replacement or issue a 100% store credit with no hassle.

2. Climate-Safe Transit Packaging

Plants are living organisms. To protect them across Indian transit lanes, we utilize:

  • Coco-Peat Hydration Sleeves: Retains root moisture for up to 6 days without waterlogging.
  • Tri-Wall Corrugated Boxes: Built with ventilation holes for air exchange while shielding against transit impact.
  • Eco-Friendly Straw & Paper Cushions: 100% biodegradable protection without plastic bubble wrap.

3. Delivery Timelines & Charges

• Orders over ₹499 qualify for FREE Climate-Safe Delivery across all serviceable Indian PIN codes.
• Standard delivery fee for orders below ₹499 is ₹50.
• Metros (Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune): 2-4 business days.
• Rest of India: 3-5 business days.

4. Non-Living Goods Returns (Pots, Stands, Tools)

Unused pots, stands, and sealed fertilizer bags may be returned within 14 days of receipt for a full refund via your original payment mode (Razorpay or Bank Transfer).
