Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Green Garden collects your name, email, delivery address, and phone number exclusively to fulfill plant shipments and provide digital care reminders. We do not sell or rent personal information to data brokers.

2. Payment Information Security

Card numbers, UPI PINs, and banking credentials are never processed or stored on our servers. All transactions are securely routed through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways (Razorpay) over 256-bit SSL encryption.

3. Plant Health & Uploaded Photos

Photos uploaded to the AI Plant Doctor are processed solely for botanical diagnosis and plant care evaluation. Users retain full ownership of their garden photographs.

4. Your Rights

You may request account deletion, data export, or unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time by contacting support@greengarden.com.
