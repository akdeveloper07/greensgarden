Mild Nitrogen & Magnesium Chlorosis

Severity
Moderate (Reversible)

The interveinal yellowing with preserved green veins indicates early magnesium deficiency compounded by restricted root aeration. Left untreated, lower leaves will drop prematurely.

Prescribed Botanical Care Plan

1
Flush Excess Salts: Water with filtered rain or settled tap water until 20% drains freely to reset soil EC.
2
Foliar Magnesium Spray: Dissolve 1 tsp pure Epsom Salt in 1 liter water and mist the undersides of leaves early in the morning.
3
Organic Feeding: Top-dress with cold-pressed Neem cake and aged humic compost to restore micronutrients.
